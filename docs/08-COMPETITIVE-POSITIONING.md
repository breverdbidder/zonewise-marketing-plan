# 08 — Competitive Positioning

## Market Landscape

Florida has TWO major auction markets: **foreclosure sales** (in-person at courthouse) and **tax deed sales** (online via realforeclose.com). Most investors participate in both. Yet every existing tool covers only one — or neither — with AI intelligence. No single platform provides AI-powered end-to-end auction intelligence across both sale types with both cloud and desktop delivery.

This fragmentation is our opportunity. Investors currently juggle 2-3 separate tools, manual research across two different auction systems, and have zero AI-powered predictions for either. ZoneWise eliminates all of that.

## Direct Competitors

### PropertyOnion ($39-99/month)
**What they do:** Foreclosure data aggregation with 8 KPIs per property
**Their strength:** Established brand, decent UI, multi-state coverage
**Their weakness:** No ML predictions, no max bid calculation, no lien analysis automation, cloud-only
**Our advantage:**
- AI-powered BID/REVIEW/SKIP recommendations (they have none)
- Max Bid Calculator with proven formula
- Automated lien priority analysis (they require manual title search)
- Desktop option for institutional clients (they're cloud-only)
- County-level demographic intelligence integration

**Positioning vs PropertyOnion:** "PropertyOnion shows you data. ZoneWise tells you what to DO with it."

### Foreclosure.com ($39.95/month)
**What they do:** National foreclosure listing aggregation
**Their strength:** National coverage, established brand, large user base
**Their weakness:** They're a listing service, not an analysis tool. No AI, no local depth.
**Our advantage:**
- We analyze; they just list
- Florida-specific depth vs national breadth
- ML predictions on outcomes
- Lien analysis that goes beyond surface data

**Positioning vs Foreclosure.com:** "They list properties. We analyze them."

### Auction.com (Free — commission model)
**What they do:** Online auction marketplace
**Their strength:** Actually hosts auctions, large inventory, trusted platform
**Their weakness:** They're a marketplace, not an intelligence tool. They make money when you bid.
**Our advantage:**
- We're advisor, not auctioneer — no conflict of interest
- We tell you what NOT to bid on (they never will)
- ML-powered risk assessment vs their basic property listings
- We cover in-person courthouse auctions (they don't)

**Positioning vs Auction.com:** "They want you to bid. We want you to bid SMART."

### RealtyTrac / ATTOM Data ($49.95/month)
**What they do:** National foreclosure and property data provider
**Their strength:** Deep data, API access, institutional client base
**Their weakness:** Data provider, not decision tool. National = no Florida-specific depth.
**Our advantage:**
- Actionable recommendations, not raw data
- Florida-specific ML models trained on actual auction outcomes
- Desktop delivery option
- Price-competitive at the individual level

**Positioning vs RealtyTrac:** "They sell data. We sell decisions."

### Manual Research (Free — costs time)
**What they do:** Investors manually check BCPAO, clerk records, tax collector, Google Maps
**Their strength:** Free, familiar, "how it's always been done"
**Their weakness:** 10+ hours per auction cycle, inconsistent, error-prone, no predictions
**Our advantage:**
- Automates 10+ hours of research into 10 minutes
- Catches liens and issues that manual research misses
- ML predictions impossible to replicate manually
- Consistent methodology across every property

**Positioning vs Manual:** "The investor next to you at the courthouse knows everything you know — plus what our AI found."
