## Core Strategy: Dual Distribution

| Channel | Target | Product | Price Range |
|---------|--------|---------|-------------|
| **B2C Cloud** | Solo investors, agents, small REI | ZoneWise.ai web app | Free → $49/mo |
| **B2B Desktop** | Title companies, law firms, funds | ZoneWise Desktop (Craft Agents fork) | $299/mo → Enterprise |

## Dual Auction Coverage

| Auction Type | Format | Platform | ZoneWise Coverage |
|-------------|--------|----------|-------------------|
| **Foreclosure Sales** | In-person at courthouse | Clerk of Court | Full AI analysis + lien priority |
| **Tax Deed Sales** | Online bidding | realforeclose.com | Full AI analysis + tax cert history |

Both auction types run through the same intelligence layer — ML predictions, property analysis, risk assessment, and max bid calculations. Two markets, one platform.

## The Moat

1. **Open Source Foundation** — Craft Agents OSS keeps infrastructure costs near zero
2. **Proprietary Intelligence** — ML models, lien analysis, Everest Ascent pipeline
3. **Free Upstream Upgrades** — Every Craft Agents OSS improvement flows into our fork
4. **Two Distribution Channels** — Same intelligence, two delivery mechanisms
5. **Desktop Stickiness** — B2B installation = high switching cost = low churn
