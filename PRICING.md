## Pricing Logic

### Why $49/month (PRO)
- **Below pain threshold** for individual investors — less than a single skip trace
- **Dual auction coverage** — foreclosures AND tax deeds in one subscription (competitors charge separately or don't cover both)
- **Monthly auction value** of even one correct BID/SKIP decision = $5K-50K in equity or avoided loss
- **Competitor benchmark:** PropertyOnion charges $39-99/month for foreclosure-only data with less intelligence
- **Target ROI messaging:** "One good decision pays for 10 years of ZoneWise"

### Why $299/month (ENTERPRISE)
- **B2B budget alignment** — title companies spend $500-2K/month on data services
- **Full auction type coverage** — firms handling both foreclosures and tax deeds need one unified system
- **Desktop delivery premium** — installed software signals enterprise-grade
- **Multi-seat value** — $299 for 5 seats = $60/user, cheaper than PRO per person
- **Annual option:** $2,990/year (2 months free) for committed accounts

### Why FREE Tier Exists (Foreclosure-Only)
- **Lead generation machine** — every free user is a qualified lead (they care about auctions)
- **Tax deed paywall** — free users see foreclosure data but tax deed access requires PRO, creating a natural upgrade trigger
- **Network effects** — more users = more data = better ML predictions
- **Conversion fuel** — the blurred scores, locked calculator, AND locked tax deed data create daily upgrade pressure
- **Market intelligence** — free user behavior tells us which counties, sale types, and features to prioritize
