### Schema Markup
- **Homepage:** Organization + SoftwareApplication
- **County pages:** LocalBusiness + Event (for auctions)
- **Blog posts:** Article + BreadcrumbList
- **Pricing page:** Product + Offer

### Technical Requirements
- Page speed < 2s (Vercel edge, optimized images)
- Mobile-first responsive
- Canonical URLs on all pages
- XML sitemap auto-generated
- Robots.txt allowing all public pages
- Open Graph + Twitter cards on every page
- Alt text on all images
- Internal linking between county pages, blog posts, and product pages

## Link Building Strategy

### Phase 1: Foundation (Month 1-3)
- Submit to real estate directories and tool lists
- Guest posts on FL real estate blogs
- HARO responses for foreclosure/real estate topics
- Social profiles (LinkedIn, Twitter, Facebook)

### Phase 2: Authority (Month 3-6)
- Data-driven content (FL foreclosure trends) that attracts press links
- Partnerships with FL real estate education providers
- Speaking at local REI meetups (generates backlinks from event pages)
- PR: "AI-powered foreclosure tool launches in Florida" angle

### Phase 3: Scale (Month 6-12)
- County-specific content attracts local links
- Integration partnerships (CRM tools, title companies)
- User-generated content and case studies
- Affiliate/referral links from satisfied users
